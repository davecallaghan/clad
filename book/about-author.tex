\chapter*{About the Author}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Author}

David Callaghan is a data and AI engineer who builds the governance,
quality, and trust infrastructure that production data systems depend on.

His work focuses on the practical problems that arise when data and AI
systems must operate reliably under real-world constraints: changing
requirements, sensitive information, distributed pipelines, and
accountable stakeholders.

CLAD grew out of that practice.  Every concept in this book was developed
against real systems, real constraints, and real delivery environments.
